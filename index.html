SCENARIO INTEL

ODDBALL: RELOADED · QUICK RULES

Pick up: Move into, within, out of or through the Oddball cube, provided no enemy model is there. It occupies the Pick-Up Weapon slot and replaces its weapon.

While carrying: No ranged weapons and no Items. Oddball melee: CC · AP 2 · ESD (1) · Lethal (1).

Drop: On carrier death, except an Assault kill transfers it to the enemy fighter. A dropped ball Scatters. If it leaves the board, return to starting position.

Reloaded exception: Ordinary Pinning and Forced Movement do not cause a drop. Successful Concussive, Rapid Fire or Suppression effects do, even against pinning-immune models.

Rounds 2–6: The Oddball returns to its original starting point at the beginning of each round.
